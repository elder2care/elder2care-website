Elder2Care Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 18, 2026
Elder2Care (“Elder2Care,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the privacy of individuals who visit, access, or use the Elder2Care website, applications, digital platforms, tools, resources, and related services, collectively referred to as the “Services.”
This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we may use and disclose that information, how we protect it, how long we may retain it, and the rights and choices available to you.
This Privacy Policy forms part of and is incorporated into the Elder2Care Terms of Service.
By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information, Elder2Care will obtain consent in accordance with applicable law.
1. Information We Collect
Personal Information
Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect personal information that identifies you directly or indirectly.
This may include:
	•	Name
	•	Email address
	•	Telephone number
	•	Account and login information
	•	Postal address or general geographic area
	•	Information concerning your relationship to an older adult or person receiving care
	•	Care preferences and support needs
	•	Accessibility requirements
	•	Communication preferences
	•	Information provided when contacting Elder2Care
	•	Payment and transaction information where applicable
	•	Information about another person that you are authorized to provide
	•	Other information voluntarily provided through the Services.
Where relevant to a particular Elder2Care service, personal information may also include information concerning health, wellbeing, daily living, mobility, accessibility, caregiving circumstances, or other information that may be considered sensitive or subject to additional legal protections.
We collect information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

Elder2Care may allow caregivers, family members, authorized representatives, or other users to provide information concerning another individual.
If you provide information about another person, you are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate authority or consent to provide that information where required by law.
Where appropriate, Elder2Care may take reasonable steps to verify authority or obtain consent directly from the individual concerned.
Location Information
Certain Elder2Care Services may use location or geographic information to help users identify resources available in their area.
Depending on the features you use, location information may be obtained from information you provide, your IP address, your device, or location services that you authorize.
Location information may be used to:
	•	Identify nearby community resources.
	•	Provide location relevant navigation services.

You may control location permissions through your device settings where supported.
Usage and Technical Information
We may automatically collect information about how you access and use the Services.
This may include:
	•	IP address
	•	Device type
	•	Operating system
	•	Browser type
	•	Device identifiers
	•	Referring website or application
	•	Pages or features accessed
	•	Dates and times of access
	•	General usage patterns
	•	Error and diagnostic information
	•	Network information
	•	Other technical information reasonably necessary to operate and secure the Services.
We may collect some of this information using cookies, pixels, logs, analytics tools, and similar technologies.
Cookies and Similar Technologies
Elder2Care may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Services, remember preferences, understand usage patterns, improve performance, maintain security, and measure the effectiveness of communications and features.
Some cookies may be necessary for the operation of the Services, while others may support analytics or personalization.
Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate notice and choices concerning non essential cookies and similar technologies.

Third party service providers may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, or similar technologies in connection with the Services.

3. Progressive Healthy Aging Services and Future Data Use
Elder2Care is intended to evolve progressively as a technology enabled healthy aging platform.
The Services may develop from responding to immediate care and support needs toward proactive planning and coordination, and eventually toward predictive and personalized healthy aging capabilities.
As the Services evolve, Elder2Care may collect and process additional categories of information that are reasonably necessary to provide such capabilities, subject to applicable law, appropriate notice, and consent where required.
Depending on the Services available at a particular time, these information sources may include:
	•	Information provided directly by individuals.
	•	Information provided by authorized caregivers or family members.
	•	Care and support preferences.
	•	Accessibility and mobility information.
	•	Community participation and social connection information.
	•	Information concerning service needs and service utilization.
	•	Geographic and community level information.
	•	Information from publicly available datasets.
	•	Information obtained from participating organizations or service providers where authorized.
	•	Information from connected devices or technologies where a user expressly enables such functionality.
	•	Health, wellbeing, functional, cognitive, mood, or other assessment information where a particular Elder2Care service is designed to collect such information and appropriate legal requirements are satisfied.
	•	Other information reasonably necessary for future healthy aging capabilities.
The purpose of collecting and processing such information may include:
	•	Improving care navigation.
	•	Identifying potential support needs earlier.
	•	Supporting proactive healthy aging planning.
	•	Personalizing community and support recommendations.
	•	Identifying patterns that may assist users and authorized caregivers in planning for changing needs.
	•	Developing predictive or risk identification capabilities where legally permitted and appropriately validated.
	•	Improving community level understanding of healthy aging needs.
	•	Developing, testing, and improving future Elder2Care services.
	•	Supporting research, analytics, planning, and innovation using appropriately governed information.
Elder2Care will not represent that a particular future capability is available unless that capability is actually offered as part of the Services.
Where a new feature requires the collection, use, or disclosure of personal information for a materially different purpose or involves a new category of sensitive information, Elder2Care will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent where required by applicable law.
