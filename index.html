What is Elder2Care?

A proactive community navigation and healthy aging platform connecting older adults, caregivers, volunteers, healthcare providers and community resources.

Who is it for?

Older adults, family caregivers, healthcare professionals, municipalities, businesses, volunteers and community organizations.

Why is it different?

We combine human compassion, community navigation and AI-enabled insight to prevent crises — not just respond to them.

How to reach us

Email info@elder2care.ca, our contact form below, or visit us in Brampton, Ontario.

About Elder2Care

A new category in healthy aging

Elder2Care is a Canadian Healthy Aging Community Navigation and Community Intelligence platform. Today, we provide proactive healthcare navigation — helping older adults and caregivers discover the right community support before problems become crises.

Our long-term vision is to become the world's first AI-powered Predictive Community Intelligence Platform for Healthy Aging — enabling communities to anticipate needs, strengthen independence, reduce isolation and improve healthy aging, rather than waiting for illness.

We bring together everyone with a role to play in healthy aging:

Community Intelligence Platform\u2122

The infrastructure behind proactive care

Five interconnected modules that turn community knowledge into timely, human action. Select a module to learn more.

01

Community Digital Twin

+
A living map of the services, people and resources available to older adults in a community — kept current so navigation is always grounded in what's actually available today.
02

Neighborhood Health Pulse

+
An aggregated, privacy-respecting view of emerging needs across a neighbourhood, helping partners direct resources where they matter most.
03

Invisible Care Network

+
The informal network of neighbours, volunteers and community members already supporting older adults — made visible, coordinated and strengthened.
04

Community Navigation Engine

+
The matching layer that connects a stated need to the right service, program or person — reducing time spent searching and calling around.
05

Healthy Aging Dashboard

+
A shared view for families, providers and partners to track progress, referrals and outcomes over time.
